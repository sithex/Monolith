## Rules
Read docs/notch-companion-characters.md and the notch-companion-characters skill. Implement and test, do not delegate. No live app/plasmoid/settings changes, deployment, process kills, commits, or skill/memory writes. Parent will audit and deploy. ## Remove Obelisk completely
Remove the selectable choice, QML registry entry and artwork file, and supported public enum/TS union member. Preserve existing saved settings: deserialize legacy `obelisk` as Blob (e.g. serde alias on Blob), rather than resetting or rejecting the entire settings document. Test legacy migration, canonical resave and QML unknown fallback. Update pack tests and render arrays to remaining four. Preserve all unrelated characters.

## Companion Cube tracking
Use existing face.shownX/shownY, existing brain event-driven pointer relay and pixel-snap helper; no pointer polling/new perpetual timer/extra Behavior. Give Cube visibly cursor-directed lean/tilt AND bounded whole-device-pixel translation so it responds across the whole attended monitor, not only hover. Use a small number of discrete rotation steps (e.g. at most +/-8 degrees) so quarter-pixel brain glide does not create dozens of redraw values. Preserve recognizable centered heart, no eyes. Stay within 20x20 slot even at all combinations of tracking, habit and hover (reduce cube extent or cap transforms as necessary); don't clip corners. Integrate finite habit with tracking without stacking excessive transform range. Freeze transformation changes on hidden/unattended/reduced motion/sleep; decide explicit baseline vs held pose and test. Hover remains distinct. Prove real left/right/up/down pointer inputs, not just manual art property mutation, reach art and cause visible image differences. Prove repeated snapped inputs cause no art transform changes. No animated path re-tessellation.

## Nurgling fidelity
Previous green two-horned rounded rectangle/imp is rejected. Fetch actual reference images and INSPECT them, not just palette/text. Primary source https://www.warhammer.com/en-US/shop/Nurglings-2017 ; official licensed Sassy/Cheeky Nurgling references also valid. Derive an original miniature rendition, do not embed downloaded artwork in UI. Choose coherent identifiable reference (prefer single crooked horn, squat/bloated silhouette, broad asymmetric toothy grin, stubby legs/clawed limbs, olive putrid flesh, conspicuous belly with navel and a couple of pustules). Distinct head/body silhouette rather than flat rounded box with ears. At actual 20px reduce detail rather than relying on subpixel marks. Hover should change grin/arm or tongue visibly; keep gaze and blink, short scratch habit. Add accurate picker preview matching final silhouette and colors. No source-code copy from another project. Save image sources/URLs and rationale in revision verification doc.
